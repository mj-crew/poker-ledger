Drop your Garage Poker logo here as:  logo.png

The app header and login screen load /logo.png automatically.
- Recommended: a square or wide PNG with a transparent background, ~200px tall.
- If no logo.png is present, the header falls back to the "Flawless Poker 9♦ 4♦" wordmark.

(You can also use logo.svg — if you prefer SVG, save it as logo.png's sibling and
change the two <img src="/logo.png"> references in web/src to /logo.svg.)
